      • As Jamie on the left, ask for your own MFA reset. Approve it on the right.
        • 
        
      • As Jamie, ask to reset Priya's MFA. It's refused before the helpdesk is asked: Jamie isn't Priya or her manager.

      What's real and what's simulated
      
      
      
        
      Real
      
        
      The model (Claude), the governance code that decides what runs, the per-call tokens and approval receipts, the audit trail, and the evaluation results.
      
        
      Simulated
      
        
      Larkspur Labs, its people, directory, devices and runbooks, and the sign-in: both sides sign in through a development token issuer, not a real identity provider. "Reset MFA" changes a flag in this session's own copy of the directory and nothing else.
