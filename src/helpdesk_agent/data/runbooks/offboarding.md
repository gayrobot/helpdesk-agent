# Offboarding

Synthetic runbook for helpdesk-agent.

## Account shutdown

People Ops schedules the shutdown. At the scheduled time the account is disabled, sessions are revoked and the person is removed from every group. Disabled accounts are never re-enabled or changed by the helpdesk; requests about them go to IT.

## Returning equipment

A prepaid return box is sent within two working days. Laptops are wiped on arrival.

## Mailbox and files

The manager gets access to the leaver's Drive files for 30 days. Email is forwarded to the manager for 30 days if they ask for it in the offboarding form.
