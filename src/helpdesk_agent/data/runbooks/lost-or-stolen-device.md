# Lost or stolen device

Synthetic runbook for helpdesk-agent.

## Immediately

Report it in #it-help or by ticket as soon as you notice. IT locks the device remotely through Jamf or Intune. If the device was stolen, also file a police report and send the reference number to IT.

## Your phone

If the lost device is the phone with Okta Verify, you need an MFA reset before you can sign in again (see the Okta Verify re-enrollment runbook). Sign out of Slack and Google on the web from another device if you can.

## Replacement

IT ships a replacement laptop within three working days. Work from the web apps meanwhile.
