# Office Wi-Fi and printing

Synthetic runbook for helpdesk-agent.

## Wi-Fi networks

`Larkspur` is the staff network: managed laptops connect automatically with a certificate. `Larkspur-Guest` is for visitors and personal phones; the password is posted at reception and changes monthly.

## Can't join the staff network

The certificate comes from device management. Make sure the laptop is compliant in Jamf or Intune, then restart it in the office.

## Printing

Printers are added automatically on managed laptops. Release a print job by tapping your badge at any printer. Jobs not released in 24 hours are deleted.
