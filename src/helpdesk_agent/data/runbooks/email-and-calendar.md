# Email and calendar

Synthetic runbook for helpdesk-agent.

## Shared mailboxes

Shared mailboxes (for example `support@example.test`) are Google Groups. The mailbox owner adds members; the helpdesk escalates requests when the owner can't be reached.

## Calendar delegation

To let an assistant manage your calendar, open Calendar settings, choose "Share with specific people", add them and pick "Make changes and manage sharing". Delegation only works between people at Larkspur Labs.

## Out of office

Set an auto-reply under Gmail settings > General > Vacation responder.

## Missing email

Check Spam and the "All Mail" label first. Messages from new external senders can be held in quarantine for up to an hour.
